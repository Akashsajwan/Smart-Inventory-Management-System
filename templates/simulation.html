🍩 1. Where Did Your Money Go? (Cost Structure Donut)

See what took up your budget: Buying goods, Delivery fees, or Storage rent.

💡
Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Colors: 🔵 Blue = Buying goods from suppliers (>90% of budget), 🟠 Orange = Delivery and shipping fees, 🟢 Green = Warehouse storage rent.

Key Insight: Purchasing constitutes >90% of total outlay. Reorder Point (ROP) minimizes holding cost through EOQ batch replenishment without risking shortages.

⏱️ 2. How Happy Were Your Customers? (Service Level Dial)

Customer satisfaction dial (100% means zero customers were disappointed).

💡
Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Guide Line (--- 95% Target Line): The red tick marker denotes the executive 95% service satisfaction threshold (passing score).

Performance: Dial readings reaching or surpassing 95% indicate near-perfect order fulfillment with zero to negligible customer stockout impact.

📈 3. How Full Was Your Warehouse? (Stock Level Trajectory)

Total units sitting on warehouse shelves each day (depletions vs. delivery arrivals).

💡
Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Guide Line (--- Safety Stock Buffer Line): The red dashed horizontal line is your danger line (like the low-fuel light in a car!). If stock drops below this, you are in danger of running out.

Dynamics: Downward slopes illustrate daily customer sales depletion; sharp upward spikes reflect vendor restock deliveries arriving into inventory.

📉 4. How Much Cash Did You Earn Each Day? (Daily Sales Revenue)

Realized sales cash flow trajectory in Indian Rupees (₹) across simulation days.

💡
Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Guide Line (--- Avg Revenue Baseline): The dotted amber line indicates your normal daily earnings baseline.

Dynamics: Curves dipping below this baseline reveal days where warehouse stockouts prevented sales and incurred estimated revenue loss.

🎯 5. Which Products Are Risky? (Product Danger Map)

Pinpoints which items take too long to arrive from suppliers or sell out too fast.

💡
Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Guide Lines (--- Crosshair Lines): Vertical & horizontal dashed lines indicate Average Lead Time and Average Daily Demand.

Risk Quadrants: 🟢 Green dots = Safe items. 🔴 Red dots = Items that ran out of stock! Top-right quadrant items take long to deliver and sell fast, so they need the largest safety buffers.

📊 6. What Was Sold vs. What Was Lost (Daily Sales & Shortages)

Side-by-side comparison of units successfully sold vs. missed sales from empty shelves.

Chart Explanation & Guide Lines

Guide Line (--- Avg Demand Target): The dotted purple line marks average daily customer demand across both policies.

Shortage Indicators: Solid bars show fulfilled sales; 🔴 red capped bars show unfulfilled lost units where stock was depleted and customers left empty-handed.
