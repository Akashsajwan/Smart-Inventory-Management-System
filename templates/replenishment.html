Understanding the OR Calculations

These formulas support inventory control and replenishment decisions.

1. Safety Stock (SS)

Buffer stock intended to protect against demand variability during lead time.

SS = Z × σ × √L
2. Reorder Point (ROP)

The inventory threshold that triggers a replenishment decision.

ROP = Average Daily Demand × Lead Time + SS
3. Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

Balances estimated ordering and annual holding costs under the basic EOQ model.

EOQ = √(2 × D × S / H)
4. Inventory Position

Includes available stock and outstanding orders, minus backorders if tracked.

Position = On Hand + On Order − Backorders
